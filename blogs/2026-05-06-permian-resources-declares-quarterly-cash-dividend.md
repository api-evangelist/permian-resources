MIDLAND, Texas – Permian Resources Corporation (“Permian Resources” or the “Company”) (NYSE: PR) today announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly base cash dividend of $0.16 per share of Class A common stock, or $0.64 per share on an annualized basis. The base dividend is payable on June 30, 2026 to shareholders of record as of June 16, 2026. About Permian Resources Headquartered in Midland, Texas, Permian Resources is an independent oil and natural gas company focused on driving peer-leading returns through the acquisition, optimization and development of high-return oil and natural gas properties.
